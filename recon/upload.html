Run a reconciliation

Drop in the ledger exports for any period, or for every period at once. Results are split by financial year, with a timeline you can expand year by year. Each file is identified by its own content, so the order you pick them in does not matter.

Each file's letterhead is checked against the company it is meant to be, and a zero-match result explains itself — missing bill numbers and non-overlapping bill numbers are different problems and are reported as such. The last result stays available at /result.
